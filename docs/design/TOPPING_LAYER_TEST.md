# Single-cup topping proof

Isolated review page: `/topping-test.html`. Existing menu and approved mobile interaction are unchanged.

Brown sugar milk tea uses three independently toggled photographic ingredient groups: irregular black grass jelly, translucent brown coffee jelly, soft yellow pudding. A foreground layer of the original liquid hides lower portions of the toppings. All eight subsets can be added and removed, with live pricing from RM11.90 to RM17.40. This is a compositing proof, not a claim of physically simulated or photograph-identical results.

Browser QA: exercised all eight subsets and checked visible layer counts and displayed prices. Desktop composition reviewed visually.

Asset generated with built-in image generation; original source preserved separately. WebP encoding preserves transparency. The three ingredients are cropped at display time using SVG symbols from one coherent sprite sheet to share camera and lighting.
